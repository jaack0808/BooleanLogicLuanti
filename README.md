#Boolean Logic Lab

##Luanti Boolean Logic Lab

- What I did in the lab, including the combination lock, two-switch light, and custom made device

##Combination Lock Light

- The first device was a four lever combination lock light
- I used the AND gate to make it so you need to use the second and fourth lever to power the door as seen in the pictures

##Combination Lock Door

- The second device was mostly like the combination lock light, but instead powering a light it powered a 2x2 piston door
- I used the same AND gate to make it so the second and third levers powered the door, when you powered both levers it would send a connection to 4 pistons and open/close the door

##Two Switch Light

- The two switch light was basically a light that you have at your house where two switches control the same light, if you turn one on, and leave it on, you can use the other switch to turn it off.
- I use the XOR gate and hooked both levers onto the same light, by doing so when you turn one switch on, it will turn the light off, and when you flick the other switch the light will turn off, and vice versa.

##Custom Device

- I mostly used the same design as the Combination lock but with two levers that power 3 pistons that push light blocks up and power them
- I used the and gate to connect the levers and send the connection to 3 pistons, on top of the pistons had light blocks that were not powered, but when the pistons extend they touch power from above and make the light blocks light up
